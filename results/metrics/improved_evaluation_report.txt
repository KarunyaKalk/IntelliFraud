==========================================================================
6. LIMITATIONS
==========================================================================
1. Training reached the maximum of 100 epochs and early stopping did not trigger. The best validation loss (0.4319) occurred at epoch 100, the final epoch. Validation loss was still decreasing slowly near the end of training (0.4329 at epoch 93 -> 0.4319 at epoch 100), so the model may not be fully converged. Further training might be worth exploring in future work; no additional training was performed for this evaluation.
2. Only 98 test frauds: confidence intervals are wide and small differences are not meaningful.
3. Same random (not time-based) split and the same Time feature as the baseline, so the time-ordering limitation still applies.
4. The threshold rule (95th percentile of legitimate validation errors) fixes the false-alarm rate on legitimate data near 5%; it is not optimised for fraud detection (by design: no labels).
5. Reproducibility: seeds and TensorFlow op determinism were set; identical results are expected on the same machine and software versions, not guaranteed across different hardware.
6. The 70 duplicate copies inside the official test set were kept, so both models are compared on the original test rows.
